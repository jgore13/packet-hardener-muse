# Task Packet — Online booking for a residential cleaning company (synthetic)

## Context
A cleaning company with 12 cleaners takes bookings by phone today. It wants an
online booking page that collects a deposit.

## Requirements
1. Customers book a cleaning slot online and pay a $40 deposit.
2. Deposits are non-refundable.
3. Customers who cancel more than 48 hours ahead get a full refund.
4. Show each cleaner a reliability score on their dashboard.
5. Each cleaner marks "background check complete" in their profile before they can be assigned jobs.

## Done when
- The booking flow feels easy.
